FONTLOG for Neha Sans and Neha Serif
====================================

This file records how Neha Sans and Neha Serif differ from the fonts they come from, and who made them.


Basic font information
----------------------

Neha Sans and Neha Serif are Kannada typefaces built from the sources of Noto Sans Kannada and Noto Serif Kannada (https://github.com/notofonts/kannada), with fixes to Kannada shaping and a new character. They cover the Kannada block and have no Latin letters.

They are licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1 (OFL.txt), with no Reserved Font Name. "Noto" is a trademark of Google, so these fonts carry another name. They are named for Neha Prasad, a founding member of Open Kannada.

Neha Sans is a variable font with weight (100 to 900) and width (62.5 to 100) axes. Neha Serif has a weight axis (100 to 900). Each also comes as static TTF and OTF files for every named instance, and as WOFF2 for the web.


ChangeLog
---------

2026-11-18 (version 1.000)
- First release as Neha Sans and Neha Serif, from the notofonts/kannada sources at commit 21adadc.
- ra + vocalic R sign (U+0CB0 U+0CC3) stays as ra with the sign, as typed. Upstream draws it as the vowel letter with a reph (notofonts/kannada#61).
- U+0CDC KANNADA ARCHAIC SHRII is added in every master. The outlines are drafts: in Neha Sans, Medium and heavier at every width close up the small 3 inside the bowl; in Neha Serif, ExtraBold and Black do the same.
- The Sans source also defines Noto Sans Kannada UI, a family with taller vertical metrics. It is not part of this release.
- Built with gftools builder (variable fonts, static TTF autohinted with ttfautohint, static OTF), then renamed: family, PostScript and file names, version 1.000, vendor ID NONE, a new unique ID, the trademark record removed, and the copyright line of The Open Kannada Authors added after the upstream one.
